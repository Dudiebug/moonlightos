# MoonlightOS v0.1.10

MoonlightOS v0.1.10 removes browsers from the distributed ISO. During the
one-time setup wizard, the owner can choose no browser, Firefox ESR, or
Chromium. The same chooser remains available in Settings, and selected browsers
are installed directly from Debian's signed repositories. Changing the default
does not silently delete an installed package, profile, bookmarks, or history.

This release also installs MoonlightOS's license, third-party notices, and
source-code instructions into the image. It includes the complete Moonlight Qt
and chiaki-ng license texts, pinned upstream source snapshots, and an exact
binary/source-version manifest for every installed Debian package. Moonlight Qt
6.1.0 and chiaki-ng 1.10.0 remain preinstalled; Tailscale remains installed but
disabled and unauthenticated by default.

The release gate builds the ISO, boots it through OVMF firmware, selects the
real installer menu entry, installs to a blank 32 GiB virtual disk, detaches the
ISO, and verifies launcher readiness and persistent state across independent
cold boots. The release remains a draft until its downloaded asset passes the
same checksum and clean-install checks.
